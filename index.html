04 / Process

From drawings to delivery

You approve the sequence before animation starts, so there are no surprises at the end.

  1. Step 1

    Brief & files

    Send what you have: CAD or BIM models (Revit, SolidWorks, STEP, IFC), drawings, product sheets or photos. We agree on who will watch it and what they need to understand.

  2. Step 2

    Storyboard & sequence

    You review a shot list and assembly order. This is where technical accuracy gets locked in.

  3. Step 3

    Animation & review

    Modeling, motion, materials and lighting, with draft renders for your feedback along the way.

  4. Step 4

    Delivery

    Final MP4s sized for where they'll play: 16:9 for presentations and YouTube, square and vertical for social, seamless loops for trade show screens.

05 / About

Build Sequence Visuals specializes in technical 3D animation for construction, engineering and manufacturing companies.

Based in Portland, OregonWorking with companies across the Pacific Northwest and beyond. Local teams can meet in person, and everyone else works with us entirely online, from first files to final delivery.

Most animation starts with how a video should look. Ours starts with how the thing actually gets built: the install order, the connections, the tolerances, and the reason step four has to happen before step five.

That comes from an engineering and fabrication background, not only a design one. We read your drawings the way your team does, ask the questions your installers would ask, and catch the steps that don't add up before they ever reach the screen.

The result is animation made by someone who knows how the parts actually go together.

  • 01

    Accurate by default

    Models are built from your CAD, BIM or shop drawings, and every sequence follows the real construction or assembly order.

  • 02

    Clear to any audience

    Owners, installers, investors and inspectors see the same thing and understand it the same way, whether or not they read drawings.

  • 03

    Easy to work with

    A clear scope up front, set review points along the way, and final files delivered in every size and format you need.
